## 2026-08-21 - Accessible Recording Controls
**Learning:** Icon-only control buttons, especially in dynamic states like activity recording (play, pause, stop), often lack inherent accessible names. This creates a critical barrier for screen reader users relying on these core actions.
**Action:** Always assign descriptive `aria-label` attributes to icon-only buttons (like MapPin, Play, Target, Heart) to ensure interactive elements are fully understandable via assistive technologies.
